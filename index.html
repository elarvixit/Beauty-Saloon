The Maison

Where beauty is considered, never rushed.

Maison Élan was founded on a simple belief: true beauty is revealed, not applied. Each visit begins with a private consultation, where our artists study your features, lifestyle and ambitions before a single tool is lifted.

Inside our sunlit atelier you'll find hand-selected formulations from the finest houses of France, Italy and Japan, served alongside champagne, rare teas and an unhurried pace.

Isabelle Laurent Founder & Creative Director
